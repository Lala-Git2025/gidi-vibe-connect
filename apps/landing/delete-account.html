Delete your account

Applies to the Gidi Connect app by Gidi Connect (com.femola.consumerapp)

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

Open the app and go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, then confirm. Deletion runs immediately.

Option 2 — By email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

Request Deletion by Email

What gets deleted

  • Your login credentials and authentication record
  • Your profile — name, username, bio, and profile photo
  • Your posts, comments, likes, polls, and poll votes
  • Your stories and all uploaded photos and videos
  • Your follows, community memberships, check-ins, RSVPs, and reviews
  • Your notification history and device push tokens

What may be retained

  • Aggregated, non-identifying statistics (for example, a venue's total daily view count) that cannot be linked back to you
  • Records we are legally required to keep, for as long as the law requires
